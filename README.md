# KAYOSI Trading CC — Website

Responsive static website package for KAYOSI Trading CC, Ballito, KwaZulu-Natal.

## Website content
- Original full KAYOSI Trading logo (`assets/kayosi-full-logo.jpg`).
- Navy, blue, gold, white and grey brand system.
- Homepage, company overview, services, sectors, logistics, PPE, corporate branding, procurement, suppliers and contact sections.
- PPE category cards now use real product-listing photographs rather than decorative symbols.
- Featured product cards for BOVA safety footwear, head protection, high-visibility wear and gloves.
- Additional brand/product cards for AECI industrial chemicals and Lasher hand tools.
- Supplier brand section includes Amrod, Jonsson Workwear, BOVA, AECI and Lasher.
- Contact details and email-preparing enquiry form.

## Accuracy and presentation
Product photographs are representative examples from published product or brand listings. They are not a live stock feed and do not imply that a specific model is currently held in KAYOSI inventory. Exact brand, model, specification, compliance requirements, price and availability must be confirmed with each quotation.

AECI's official product range includes industrial chemicals and product areas serving construction and infrastructure. Lasher's official product catalogue includes hand tools such as spades, shovels and wheelbarrows. Website copy describes these as product ranges KAYOSI can assist customers to source, not as proof of formal appointment or exclusive distribution.

Supplier logos and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. The website's supplier section does not claim exclusive or formally appointed partnerships.

## Publish to GitHub Pages
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload the contents of the `kayosi-complete` folder (not the outer folder) into the root of the existing `kayosi` repository.
3. Replace the previous `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`, `README.md`, `robots.txt` and `assets` files as prompted.
4. Keep GitHub Pages set to branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`.
5. Refresh the published site after the Pages build completes.
