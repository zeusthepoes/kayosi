ABOUT KAYOSI TRADING

Built around what

We believe good business relationships are built on understanding the requirement, sourcing the right solution and following through.

Based in Ballito, KwaZulu-Natal, Kayosi Trading CC is positioned to support a diverse customer base — from growing businesses and established companies to institutions, contractors and public-sector buyers — with day-to-day supply and project-related requirements.

Our areas of supply include workplace products, branded apparel, safety equipment, storage systems, procurement categories and selected infrastructure materials. We value transparent communication, considered sourcing and practical delivery planning, with solutions shaped around each customer’s specifications and needs.
